# SIP-vs-Lump-Sum-Investment-Analysis
Project Overview
A quantitative study comparing systematic installment investing
(SIP-style contributions) with a one-time lump-sum investment across
Indian equity mutual fund categories over the period January 2015 to
December 2025.

The analysis uses historical NAV data from nine mutual fund schemes
across large-cap, mid-cap and small-cap categories, covering HDFC, ICICI
Prudential and SBI schemes. It compares a total investment of ₹13.2
lakh under two approaches:
- Installment approach: ₹10,000 invested each month.
- Lump-sum approach: the full ₹13.2 lakh invested at the start of
  the period.
Methodology
The research evaluates investment performance and risk using:
- Annualised returns
- Standard deviation and downside deviation
- Beta
- Sharpe and Sortino ratios
- Treynor ratio
- Jensen's Alpha
- Paired-sample t-tests
The study is based on historical NAV analysis and a
consistent-investment comparison. Its findings should be interpreted in
the context of the selected schemes and study period; historical
performance does not guarantee future returns.
Repository Contents
- Research Report: Full research methodology, analysis and
  findings.
- AI & Originality Verification Report: Supplementary report
  containing the originality-check results associated with the
  research.
Research Focus
Key question: How do installment-based and lump-sum investment
strategies compare across mutual fund categories when evaluated using
comparable total capital and risk-adjusted performance measures?
